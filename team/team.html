We are recruiting!

Graduate students: we are looking for highly motivated and hard-working students to carry out research in the field of low-dimensional solid state systems. Interested in joining? Learn more about the CMU Physics PhD Program and reach out to us.

Undergraduate students: contact us if you want to learn (or just explore for fun…) the exciting physics of novel condensed matter systems and their atomically thin nanodevices — advanced nanodevice fabrication, epitaxial growth, vdW heterostructure assembly, quantum magneto-transport, spintronics devices, optical and photoemission spectroscopy, magnetization dynamics, and surface science techniques.
